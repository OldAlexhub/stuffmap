# Privacy Policy for StuffMap

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 13, 2026

---

## Overview

StuffMap is a fully offline, private storage tracker app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how StuffMap handles your information.

**The short version:** StuffMap collects nothing. All data you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to see your data — because we never receive it.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We collect no information.**

StuffMap does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or any other data from your device to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Specifically, StuffMap does **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
- Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, device ID)
- Location data
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Crash or diagnostic reports
- Photos or media files beyond what you explicitly add to the app
- Any behavioral or tracking data

---

## 2. Data You Create in the App

When you use StuffMap, you may create:

- **Locations** — names and descriptions of storage places
- **Containers** — names, descriptions, photos, and tags for storage containers
- **Items** — names, quantities, categories, photos, notes, and tags for stored items

**All of this data is stored exclusively on your Android device**, in the app's private local storage using Android's standard app data storage system.

This data:
- Never leaves your device unless you choose to export it
- Is never transmitted to any server
- Is never accessible to us or any third party
- Is never used for advertising or analytics

---

## 3. Photos and Media

If you add photos to containers or items, those photos are referenced from your device's local storage. StuffMap does not upload photos anywhere. Photos remain on your device at all times.

---

## 4. Exported Data

StuffMap allows you to export your inventory as:

- **CSV files** — saved to your device's Downloads folder
- **HTML reports** — saved to your device's Downloads folder
- **JSON backups** — saved to your device's Downloads folder

When you choose to export, the file is saved locally on your device. If you then use Android's share feature to send the file to another app (email, messaging, cloud storage), that sharing is governed by the privacy policies of those third-party apps. StuffMap itself does not transmit the file anywhere.

---

## 5. App Permissions

StuffMap may request the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Why It Is Used |
|---|---|
| `CAMERA` | To take photos of containers and items directly within the app |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` | To select existing photos from your gallery (Android 13+) |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | To select existing photos from your gallery (Android 12 and below) |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | To save exported reports to your Downloads folder (Android 9 and below) |

These permissions are used **only** for the features described above. No data accessed through these permissions is transmitted anywhere.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

StuffMap does not integrate with or use any third-party services, including:

- No analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
- No crash reporting services (Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
- No advertising networks
- No cloud storage providers (Firebase, AWS, Google Cloud, etc.)
- No social media integrations
- No payment processors (the app is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play)

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

StuffMap does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for use by all ages.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed.

**To delete your data:**

- **Delete all data within the app:** Go to Settings → Clear All Data
- **Export a backup first:** Go to Reports → JSON Backup before clearing
- **Uninstall the app:** When you uninstall StuffMap, Android automatically deletes all app data stored by the app, including all your locations, containers, items, and settings

We have no copy of your data and cannot delete it on your behalf, because we never receive it.

---

## 9. Data Security

Because StuffMap stores all data locally on your device, the security of your data is tied to the security of your Android device. We recommend:

- Using a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) on your device
- Keeping your Android OS up to date
- Exporting and securely storing regular backups via Reports → JSON Backup
